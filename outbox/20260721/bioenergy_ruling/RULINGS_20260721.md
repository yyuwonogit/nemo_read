## ERRATUM — 2026-07-22 — §6 structural creates, `Rice Straw` / `Used Cooking Oil`

**Our error. §6 above stands as written for the historical record; the two
`Bagasse` mirrors in it are wrong and must not be executed as issued.**

§6 instructs our modelling lead to create `Resources\Primary\Rice Straw` and
`Resources\Primary\Used Cooking Oil` as flat depth-3 leaves **mirroring
`Bagasse`**. The branch *shape* in that instruction is right; the *unit* it
carries is not.

**What was wrong.** `Resources\Primary\Bagasse : Maximum Production` carries
unit **`Terawatt-hour`** — verified against canon,
[current_expressions_resources_4scenarios.csv](../../../inject/fossil/structure_handover_20260703/current_expressions_resources_4scenarios.csv)
lines 125-136 and 137-144 (v0.67 export; unit is structural, so canon-stable,
but the *values* on those rows may be stale against the live v0.76+ area).
Corroborated by [LEAP_STRUCTURE_ANATOMY.md](../../../LEAP%20structure/LEAP_STRUCTURE_ANATOMY.md)
§13.2, which splits `Maximum Production` units as "TWh 8 (renewables/biomass),
Metric Tonne 7 (5 crops + Molasses + POME — raw-crop-tonnes convention)".

The team's values for both branches are **raw tonnes** — e.g. Myanmar Rice
Straw `Interp(2025, 3.78759e+07, …)`. Cloning `Bagasse` would land 3.79e7 in a
`Terawatt-hour` field. The `unit` column is never read at inject time, so this
would have committed **silently** at ~1e7× the intended magnitude.

**Corrected instruction — mirror `Resources\Primary\Palm Oil Mill Effluent`.**

| Branch to create | Template | `Maximum Production` unit |
|---|---|---|
| `Resources\Primary\Rice Straw` | `Resources\Primary\Palm Oil Mill Effluent` | `Metric Tonne` |
| `Resources\Primary\Used Cooking Oil` | `Resources\Primary\Palm Oil Mill Effluent` | `Metric Tonne` |

`Cellulosic Rice Straw` (process, mirroring its `Cassava` sibling) is
**unaffected** — that instruction was correct.

**Why `Palm Oil Mill Effluent` and not one of the 5 crop branches.** POME's
variable panel is **byte-identical to Bagasse's** — the same 15 variables, in
the same order, per [resources_tree.txt](../../../LEAP%20structure/trees/resources_tree.txt)
lines 2 and 21 — so the shape §6 asked for is preserved exactly. What changes
is only the `Maximum Production` unit: `Metric Tonne`, which is what the team's
tonnage values require.

The 5 crop branches (`Palm Oil`, `Coconut Oil`, `Sugarcane`, `Cassava`, `Corn`)
are **not** the right template despite also being `Metric Tonne`: each
additionally carries `Area Harvested` and `Crop Yield`, and canon derives their
`Maximum Production` from those two (`Area Harvested[Thousand ha] * 1000 *
Crop Yield[t/ha]`, anatomy §13.2). Rice Straw and Used Cooking Oil are
residues/by-products with no harvested area of their own; cloning a crop branch
would create two variables nobody authors. `Molasses` is an equally valid
template — same panel, same unit — if POME is inconvenient for any reason.

**Nothing else in §6 changes.** No re-send is required from the bioenergy team;
this is a correction to an instruction that never left our side of the pipeline.
