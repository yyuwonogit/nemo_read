This is the one-page spec for pasting the historical road-fleet Stock patch. It
is a **hard input we author** (not overflow-derived) and it **replaces** the
corrupted live Current-Accounts Stock series (audit A4 / A6a / A6b). Read it
alongside `RESPONSE_TO_INJECT_20260721.md`, which carries the context.

## What it is

The observed/modelled road fleet, 2005–2024, per (region × vehicle × fuel), as a
`Stock` **Data()** series. Historical stock is our hard input and is
**scenario-invariant** — Current Accounts = ATS = BAS = RAS on those years — so
it is authored **once, under Current Accounts only** (Stock is a CA-only
variable in the canon; the scenarios inherit it).

## How to paste

- Paste each row's expression onto the per-AMS `Stock` slot under Current
  Accounts, **overwriting** whatever per-region expression is currently there.
  Real AMS regions override the `Base Template` formula — usually with a Data()
  series (the corrupted one), occasionally still with the formula for
  never-populated fuels (e.g. Bus Hydrogen). Either way, overwrite it with the
  patch expression; leave the `Base Template` row itself untouched.
- **Expression format:** `Data(2005, v, 2006, v, …, 2024, v)` in absolute
  vehicles. The CSV `expression` field carries a ` ? <provenance>` tail per our
  canonical schema — the injector splits on ` ? ` and keeps the left side as the
  LEAP expression (the `Data(...)`), the right side as the source note. Nothing
  extra to strip by hand.
- **Explicit zeros are intentional.** Configured-but-empty fuels (e.g. a fuel
  with no fleet in early years, or Bus Hydrogen throughout) carry an explicit
  `Data(…, 0)` series so no stale live value survives underneath.

## Consistency guarantees (so you can trust the paste)

- **Sums to your base-year anchor.** For every (AMS, vehicle), the sum of the
  per-fuel Stock at **2024** equals the `BaseYear_StockData\<Vehicle>` fleet we
  shipped as V5 (verified, ratio 1.000). The patch and V5 are the same fleet,
  split by fuel.
- **Class-distinct and clean.** Each vehicle class has its own series — this is
  what closes the A4 class-paste inflation and the A6 IDN-2015 ÷129.4 splice.
- **2024 anchored to published national totals.** Per-AMS and per-(AMS, vehicle)
  fleet totals are monotone and dip-free 2005–2024 (endpoint-anchored
  smoothing). Note that **individual per-fuel series may decline** — 13 of the
  160 do — where the fuel mix shifts (NG/diesel giving way to EV) or where the
  2024 published-total anchor re-splits the fleet across fuels. A declining
  per-fuel row is expected, not a defect; the class total it rolls up into is
  still monotone.

## What this does NOT change

- **Forward stock (2025–2060) stays yours** — LEAP derives it via stock-overflow
  (base-year Stock + Sales). We treat it as review-only and do not reconcile it;
  the earlier V-7 cross-check is withdrawn. This patch touches historical CA only.
- No new branches, fuels, or variables — writes only to existing `Stock` slots.
