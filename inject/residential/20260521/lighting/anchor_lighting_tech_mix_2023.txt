METHOD SIDECAR — anchor_lighting_tech_mix_2023.py
==================================================
Per-AMS 2023 residential-lighting technology-mix anchor for the LEAP
electricity-lighting five-technology tree (LEAP-tree remap, 2026-05-20).

Output: Outputs/lighting_tech_mix_2023.csv (10 rows, ASEAN-10).


1. WHY THIS ARTEFACT EXISTS
---------------------------
The LEAP Lighting sub-tree (Demand\Residential\Projections\Lighting\
Electricity\<Tech>) is authored as a FIVE-technology stack:
Incandescent, CFL, Fluorescent, Halogen, LED. Each tech leaf carries an
`Activity Level` = the % of grid-lit households using that technology;
the five shares sum to ~100% per (region, year). LEAP then derives
`Final Energy Intensity` itself from BulbsPerHH x Bulb Wattage x
LightingHours (ref: .mailbox/LIGHTING_TEAM_HANDOVER_20260520.md §2-§4).

Our upstream catalogue (Raw/lighting_catalogue_v1.csv) is LED-only
(LED_L / LED_T / LED_SL ~ 99% of rows) classified into efficacy bands.
It can describe the efficacy distribution WITHIN LED but cannot, on its
own, produce a five-technology penetration split. The base-year mix is
therefore an EXTERNAL input, encoded declaratively in this script and
evolved by build_lighting_tech_shares.py.

1. UNEP U4E — ASEAN Regional Lighting Market Assessment.
   - 2020 edition live; 2026 refresh pending (user flagged 2026-04-22).
   - Primary technology-penetration source (lamp-stock shares).
   - URL: https://united4efficiency.org/resources/  (ASEAN regional
     lighting assessment under U4E country/regional assessments).
2. IEA — Southeast Asia Energy Outlook 2022.
   - Directional sanity: region-wide LED scale-up narrative.
   - URL: https://www.iea.org/reports/southeast-asia-energy-outlook-2022
3. AEO-8 (ASEAN Energy Outlook 8th Edition, ACE 2023) + ACE (2026)
   lighting product compilation.
   - Sanity ballpark for LED dominance; the ACE 2026 *supply* base is
     LED-dominant (the residential catalogue this sector ingests).
   - URL: https://aseanenergy.org/publications/the-7th-asean-energy-outlook/
     (AEO-8 successor index; replace with AEO-8 direct link on file).

Per-AMS national-statistics secondaries used as plausibility checks
(same set as the kWh/HH anchor, anchor_lighting_intensity_2023.txt):
PLN (IDN), Suruhanjaya Tenaga / ST (MYS), EGAT / DEDE (THA), EMA (SGP),
DOE LEEP (PHL), EVN (VNM), EdC (KHM), EDL (LAO), DoS (MMR).


3. METHOD
---------
Declarative: per-AMS five-technology base-year shares (fractions, sum
to 1.0) encoded in the TECH_MIX dict. No computation from the catalogue
— the catalogue cannot yield non-LED shares.

Mix logic (income / urbanisation gradient):
- LED is the single largest share in every AMS (LED-dominant supply
  base since ~2020).
- Richer / more-urbanised AMS sit higher on the LED-adoption S-curve:
  SGP 80% > BRN 72% > MYS 65% > THA 62% > VNM 55% = PHL 55% >
  IDN 52% > KHM 45% > LAO 42% > MMR 40%.
- Residual non-LED stock is CFL + linear fluorescent (T8 tubes,
  historically dominant in kitchens / corridors), with a small
  declining incandescent tail and a small halogen share (directional
  spots; never large in residential ASEAN).

Every row: source_tier = "triangulated-placeholder", refresh_needed =
True. These are defensible placeholders bracketed by the three sources,
NOT survey-direct values. The U4E-2026 refresh is a values-only update
(swap shares + source_primary, flip refresh_needed; no schema change).


4. KEY ASSUMPTIONS
------------------
- LED-dominant base: consistent with DESIGN.md §5.1 (the residential
  product-supply base is already LED-dominant by 2025).
- Halogen small everywhere (2-4%): residential ASEAN uses little
  halogen; mostly directional / decorative.
- Population-weighted for heterogeneous AMS (esp. IDN: urban LED
  majority, rural retains more CFL / incandescent).
- MMR on a pre-2021 baseline; post-2021 disruption not reflected
  (widest uncertainty; mirrors the kWh/HH anchor caveat).


5. VALIDATION REGIME (hard-fail unless noted)
---------------------------------------------
- Required columns present.
- ASEAN-10 exact coverage (no extra, none missing).
- Five shares sum to 1.0 per AMS (|sum-1| < 1e-9).
- Each share in [0, 1].
- LED is the single largest share per AMS (mix design rule).
- source_tier in {U4E-direct, U4E-interpolated, triangulated-placeholder}.
- refresh_needed non-null bool.
- Non-empty source_primary per row.

Cross-checked downstream by Residential/Lighting/_sanity.py Level 6
(tech_mix:*).

7. REFRESH PROCEDURE (when U4E-2026 lands)
------------------------------------------
1. Replace the per-AMS share_* values in TECH_MIX with the U4E-2026
   penetration numbers.
2. Update source_primary to cite the 2026 edition; set source_tier to
   "U4E-direct" (or "U4E-interpolated" for interpolated AMS).
3. Flip refresh_needed to False per refreshed AMS.
4. Re-run anchor -> build_lighting_tech_shares -> _sanity.
5. Commit as a tech-mix v2 update (values-only; no schema change).
