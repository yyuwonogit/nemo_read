APAEC target tracking — AEO-9 v0.71

ASEAN aggregate (10 member states) · scenarios BAS / ATS / RAS · dashed line = APAEC 2030 target

Three APAEC indicators against the model's three scenarios: Baseline (BAS), AMS Target (ATS), and Regional Aspiration (RAS). Nuclear counted as renewable per direction. Energy intensity uses TPES (Total Primary Energy Supply) ÷ real GDP (PPP), indexed to 2005.

Read with care — the RAS energy-intensity line. RAS shows the smallest EI reduction despite being the most ambitious scenario. That is a TPES-accounting effect: RAS carries far more nuclear + non-combustible renewables, which enter TPES at their full primary-energy content, inflating primary supply (RAS 92 EJ vs BAS 67 EJ in 2050). The physical decarbonisation still shows in charts 1 & 2; EI defined on TPES simply penalises the RE/nuclear-heavy path.

Method. Capacity RE% = renewable generation capacity ÷ total (storage & Unmet-Load excluded; nuclear counted RE). TPES RE% from the Primary-Supply result (primary carriers only). EI reduction = 1 − (TPES/GDP)ₜ / (TPES/GDP)₂₀₀₅; TPES back-interpolated to 2005 from 2010–2020; GDP = Real GDP PPP from the Keys canon (2005–2024 history, scenario growth to 2060). Sources: v_0.71 Power / Resources Result.xlsx, keys canon v0.67. Values are the model's outputs, not observed statistics.
