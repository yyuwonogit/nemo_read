apply_higheff_kwh_drift.py — method sidecar (2026-08-13)
=========================================================

WHY
---
User direction 2026-08-13: the canonical inject economics should show High_eff
drifting toward the cheapest total cost (annualized capex + electricity opex)
in every size class by end of horizon, achieved by lowering High_eff kWh/yr
from 2025 on — WITHOUT crossing the thermodynamic (ultimate-frontier) limit.
Pre-patch state (frozen kWh): AC High_eff already TCO-cheapest in 27/30
Country x Size cells at 2060 (BAS) but Fridge in 0/30 (Mid_eff dominated).

WHAT
----
In-place patch of the two canonical injects (post-2026-07-15-fix versions):
  Residential/LEAP Input/ac_leap_inject.csv
  Residential/LEAP Input/fridge_leap_inject.csv
High_eff rows only, all three scenarios (technology characteristic — the
scenario delta stays purely compositional):
  kwh_unit(High, y) = kwh_2024(High) * (1 - rate)^max(0, y - 2025),
  floored at kwh_2024(High) * DF_ULT (ultimate frontier, never crossed).
Low_eff / Mid_eff kWh stay frozen (unchanged).

RATES (the key assumption)
--------------------------
Chosen as the MINIMUM rate reaching the frontier-feasible MAXIMUM of
"High_eff TCO-cheapest by 2060" cells (rate scan 0.5-3.0%/yr, 2026-08-13):
  AC     0.5%/yr  -> factor 0.839 by 2060; 28/30 cells flip. Equals the
                     documented autonomous-technology rate in
                     ac_leap_drift_method.md. Faster rates flip nothing more.
  Fridge 2.0%/yr  -> factor 0.493 by 2060 = EXACTLY the documented ultimate
                     frontier DF_ULT = (1-0.02)^35 in fridge_leap_drift_method.md
                     (the "RAS best at 2060" frontier). The frontier cap binds:
                     faster rates are floored and flip nothing more. 22/30 cells.

HOLDOUTS (thermodynamically/economically unreachable within the limit)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Cells where High_eff is still not TCO-cheapest at 2060 — all in low-tariff
markets where opex is too small for any kWh cut to offset the capex premium:
  AC (2):     Brunei Medium, Brunei Small (tariff 0.0072 USD/kWh)
  Fridge (8): Brunei Large/Medium/Small, Lao PDR Medium, Malaysia Large/Medium,
              Myanmar Medium, Viet Nam Medium

ENCODING (mirrors the drift-variant useful-energy anchor)
---------------------------------------------------------
  Useful EI (per Country x Size, all years/scenarios)
      = kwh_2024(High) * factor_2060 / 11630   [TOE]
  Efficiency(cell, y) = Useful_EI * 11630 / kwh_unit(cell, y) * 100
      -> High_eff ramps (AC 83.9% -> 100% at 2060; Fridge 49.3% -> 100%),
         Low/Mid constant below; never exceeds 100%.
  LEAP identity Final = Useful / Efficiency validated to 1e-6 in the script.
  om_electricity_usd = kwh_unit * tariff (recomputed).
  Demand Cost: RAS rows = capital + drifted O&M (recomputed); BAS/ATS rows
  stay capital-only per the rowwise method — unchanged.
UNCHANGED: all Activity/share columns, prices, annualized capital, CRF,
tariffs, unit_capacity_kw (service capacity does not drift), exo devices,
fridge_sales_projection.csv (upstream analytic keeps frozen kWh).

RESULT (2060, BAS and ATS identical counts)
-------------------------------------------
  AC:     High_eff TCO-cheapest 28/30 cells (was 27 BAS / 28 ATS)
  Fridge: High_eff TCO-cheapest 22/30 cells (was 0 BAS / 3 ATS-2060)

ADDENDUM 4 2026-08-14 (rev-4: ACT tier-flip fix)
------------------------------------------------
Rev 3's flat per-GJ Variable OM rate was WRONG in basis: LEAP charges it on
device OUTPUT (useful energy), which this encoding makes identical across
tiers — so tier O&M equalized, only capex differed, and ACT's optimizer bought
Low/Mid (ACFRIDGE.xlsx: ACT 2060 fridge demand 148 M GJ vs BAS 103 / ATS 89).
Rev 4: rate(tier, y) = tariff / (Efficiency(tier, y)/100) x (1000/3.6) USD/GJ
-> per device-year equals tariff x final_kWh(tier, y) exactly (validated:
IDN fridge Large High 50.37->24.84 with drift, Mid 61.11, Low 76.77 USD/yr;
Low pays 3.09x High per unit of service at 2060). Low/Mid constants, High_eff
declining Interp. LESSON (canon): per-energy device-panel costs are charged on
OUTPUT — tier-differentiate them by dividing by tier Efficiency; a flat rate
deletes the efficiency signal.
